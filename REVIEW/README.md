## What the review is asked to assess

The reviewer is asked to assess three artifacts:

1. `ARCHITECTURAL_HYPOTHESIS.md` — the hypothesis and its four clauses.
2. `research/RESPONSIBILITY_BOUNDARIES.md` — the boundary map and the four
   high-risk overlaps.
3. `FALSIFICATION/FALSIFICATION_PLAN.md` — the four failure conditions and
   the decision rule.

The reviewer is **not** asked to accept or reject AetherMemory. The reviewer
is asked whether the hypothesis, the boundary map, and the plan are
sufficient to support a final decision.
