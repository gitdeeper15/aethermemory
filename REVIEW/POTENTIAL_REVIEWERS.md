## 1. Reviewer criteria

A reviewer is suitable if:

- they are independent of the author (not Samir Baladi);
- they have no stake in AetherPolicy or AetherAudit (or, if they do, they
  declare it);
- they are able to assess an architectural argument;
- they are willing to sign `INDEPENDENCE_DECLARATION.md`.
